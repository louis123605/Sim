HOW TO READ THESE RESULTS

Title %, top-four %, and relegation % are observed frequencies across independent simulated seasons. Mean points is the average simulated final points total. These are model-based estimates, not real-world betting odds or verified forecasts.

MODEL & METHODOLOGY

Transparent prototype · illustrative strength ratings · not live or calibrated to current league data.

Competition: 20-club English top-flight illustrative dataset. Double round-robin schedule: 38 matches per club, 380 per season. The teams are an illustrative selection, not a verified live season roster.

Match engine: Independent Poisson-distributed home and away goals, with expected goals based on relative club strength and home advantage. Scores determine points, goal difference, and league positions.

Monte Carlo: Every season restarts from the same strength assumptions, independently drawing a new set of match outcomes. Frequencies estimate probabilities conditional on these assumptions.

Rare-event mode: Simulates fresh seasons until the selected target is observed or the attempt limit is reached. If an outcome is not found, that does not prove it is impossible. The observed attempts-to-first-hit is not a reliable standalone estimate of event probability.

Reproducibility: Pseudorandom generator initialized with a user-provided seed. Exports include model version, seed, settings, and sampled outcomes.

Limitations: No injuries, fixture congestion, tactical changes, player-level data, UEFA competitions, promotions, transfers, or live club-strength updates. This is not Aaron W's private model, and it is not an exact replica of FusionSim. To make forecasts credible, the ratings must be replaced with documented licensed data and validated against held-out seasons.
